Organizer Privacy Policy

Effective date: [DATE TO CONFIRM]

Organizer is an app for keeping track of your cosmetics and skincare: what you own, what you have opened, and when it expires. Organizer is developed and published by amboring ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app uses and why. Organizer is available on iOS and Android.

What we store

To sign in, Organizer uses your Google or Apple account. From that sign-in we store:

  • your account ID, which we create;
  • the email address and name the provider gives us, shown in the app and used only for your account;
  • your products, stock counts, opened items and their dates, and any notes you write.

Your products and opened items are stored on our server so they follow your account across devices. A copy is kept on your phone so the app works offline. Signing out clears that copy.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for marketing.

Sign-in tokens

Organizer keeps your sign-in tokens on your device, in the iOS Keychain or the Android app's private storage.

Deleting your account

In Settings, choose Delete account and confirm. This deletes your account, your products and your opened items from our server, and clears the copy on your phone. Deletion is permanent.

Advertising

Organizer shows ads provided by Google AdMob. Showing ads is the only thing the app uses the internet for besides your account and inventory. To deliver and measure ads, Google may collect and use information such as:

  • device identifiers, including the advertising ID on Android and the IDFA on iOS (only if you allow tracking);
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • information about the device, the app, and how you interact with ads;
  • diagnostic and performance data.

This information is collected by Google, not by us, and is handled under Google's policies.

Your choices

  • Consent: if you are in the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland, or a US state with applicable privacy laws, Organizer asks for your consent choices through Google's consent form before ads are personalized. If consent cannot be gathered, ads are not shown.
  • iOS tracking: on iPhone, Organizer asks for permission before your IDFA is used. You can change this anytime in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Android advertising ID: you can reset or delete it in your device's Settings > Privacy > Ads (the exact path varies by device).

Contact

Questions about this policy, or requests to delete your messages? Email guauguau.app@gmail.com.

Children

Organizer is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
