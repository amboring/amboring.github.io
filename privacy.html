Androp Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 6, 2026

Androp is an app for sending files between nearby iPhone and Android devices. Androp is developed and published by amboring ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app uses and why. Androp is available on iOS and Android.

Your files stay between your devices

Files you send with Androp go directly from one device to the other over your local Wi-Fi network or a hotspot that one of the devices creates. They are never uploaded to the internet, stored on any server, or visible to us. Androp has no accounts and no sign-in, and the app does not collect your name, email address, contacts or the contents of your files.

Permissions the app uses

  • Camera: only to scan the pairing QR code shown on the other device. No photos or video are saved or sent anywhere.
  • Local network / nearby Wi-Fi devices: to find and connect to the other device, or to create a hotspot for the transfer. Android's nearby-devices permission is not used to determine your location.
  • Photo library (add only, iOS): to save photos and videos you receive.
  • Notifications (Android): to show the status of a transfer while it is running.

Advertising

Androp shows ads provided by Google AdMob. Showing ads is the only thing the app uses the internet for, and ads never affect a transfer. To deliver and measure ads, Google may collect and use information such as:

  • device identifiers, including the advertising ID on Android and the IDFA on iOS (only if you allow tracking);
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • information about the device, the app, and how you interact with ads;
  • diagnostic and performance data.

This information is collected by Google, not by us, and is handled under Google's policies. Learn more at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • Consent: if you are in the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland, or a US state with applicable privacy laws, Androp asks for your consent choices through Google's consent form before ads are personalized. If consent cannot be gathered, ads are not shown.
  • iOS tracking: on iPhone, Androp asks for permission before your IDFA is used. You can change this anytime in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Android advertising ID: you can reset or delete it in your device's Settings > Privacy > Ads (the exact path varies by device).
  • If you decline personalized ads, you may still see non-personalized ads.

If you contact us

The app's Report an issue button opens your own email app. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write, and use them only to reply to you. We don't share them or add you to any list. Ask us anytime to delete your messages.

Children

Androp is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
